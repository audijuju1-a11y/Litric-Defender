## limitations (unchanged)

  * 30 seconds per target is a regression check, not a soak.
  * No AddressSanitizer/UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer on this host.
  * No structure-aware mutator; libFuzzer mutates bytes.
  * Parser process isolation is not implemented.
  * Corpus files are evidence, not product input and not checked in.
